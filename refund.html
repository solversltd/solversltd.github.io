Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025

This Refund Policy explains the conditions under which SOLVERS LTD may issue refunds for services rendered. As a professional services company, our work involves time, expertise, and third-party costs — please read this policy carefully before engaging our services.

SOLVERS LTD provides professional services, not physical products. All refund requests are evaluated on a case-by-case basis in accordance with this policy and applicable Bulgarian consumer law.

1. General Principle

Because our services involve professional time and expertise that cannot be recovered once delivered, refunds are not automatically available once work has commenced. However, we are committed to fair and transparent resolution of any payment disputes.

We encourage all clients to raise concerns as early as possible during the engagement so that issues can be resolved before they escalate to a refund request.

2. Eligibility for a Refund

A refund may be issued in the following circumstances:

  • Service not started: If you have made a payment and no work has commenced, you are entitled to a full refund of that payment, minus any applicable transaction fees.
  • Partial delivery: If we are unable to deliver a material portion of agreed services due to reasons within our control, we will issue a proportional refund corresponding to the undelivered portion.
  • Billing error: If you were charged incorrectly or charged for a service not included in your agreement, we will correct the error and issue a full refund of the overcharged amount.
  • Service cancellation by SOLVERS LTD: If we cancel an engagement without cause and before delivery, any prepaid amounts for undelivered services will be refunded in full.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • Services have been fully delivered as described in the service agreement
  • The client has provided incorrect, incomplete, or misleading information that affected the outcome
  • The client has changed business direction, decided not to proceed, or no longer requires the service after work has begun
  • Delays or unsatisfactory results caused by the client's failure to meet their obligations (e.g., late document submission)
  • External factors outside SOLVERS LTD's control, including changes in platform policies (such as Amazon), regulatory changes, or third-party service failures
  • Retainer fees covering a period during which services were available and accessible to the client

4. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request, please contact us at anastasiia.chaban@yahoo.com with the following information:

  • Your full name and company name (if applicable)
  • The invoice number or payment reference
  • The date of payment
  • A clear description of the reason for the refund request

We will acknowledge your request within 3 business days and provide a decision within 10 business days of receiving all necessary information.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be returned to the original payment method used for the transaction. Processing times vary depending on your bank or payment provider:

  • Bank transfers: 3–7 business days
  • Card payments via Stripe: 5–10 business days

SOLVERS LTD is not responsible for delays caused by your financial institution. We will provide confirmation once the refund has been initiated on our end.

6. Disputes

If you disagree with our refund decision, you may escalate the matter by sending a written appeal to anastasiia.chaban@yahoo.com. Appeals will be reviewed by senior management within 10 business days.

If a resolution cannot be reached, disputes are subject to the governing law and jurisdiction provisions set out in our Terms & Conditions — specifically, the laws of the Republic of Bulgaria and the courts of Sofia.

For payments processed through Stripe, you also have the right to raise a dispute through Stripe's dispute resolution process, in accordance with their terms of service.

7. Consumer Rights

If you are a consumer (an individual purchasing services outside of a business context) within the European Union, you may have additional rights under EU consumer protection law, including Directive 2011/83/EU. Where applicable, we comply fully with these protections.

Notably, the right of withdrawal for digital or professional services may be waived when the service has been fully performed with your prior express consent. We will inform you of this at the time of engagement if applicable.

For any questions about this Refund Policy or to initiate a refund request, contact us at anastasiia.chaban@yahoo.com. We aim to resolve all matters fairly and promptly.
